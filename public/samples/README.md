# Sample documents (fictional)

All files here are invented for judges and testers — no real people, accounts or companies.

- `bank-statement-oct-2026.csv` — a month of card transactions. Hidden anomalies: a duplicate subscription charge, a 1,899 USD purchase at 03:47 from an overseas shop, a transfer to an unknown payee, two night-time ATM withdrawals abroad with a fee, and a subscription that tripled in price.
- `receipts-business-trip.txt` — trip receipts. Hidden anomalies: the same dinner charged twice, an unusually large tip, a 03:10 minibar charge.
- `apartment-lease-excerpt.txt` — lease clauses. Hidden problems: unlimited daily late fee, entry without notice, rent increase on 7 days notice, tenant pays structural repairs, 120-day auto-renewal.

In the app: tap the 📄 button → "Try a sample", then ask the knight, e.g. "What looks unusual?"

Our assistant cannot leak what you tell it

Most website chatbots ask you not to share medical details — because conversations are logged by an AI vendor. Ours is built so that there is nothing to log.

  • Runs inside hardware-protected enclaves (TEE) on NEAR AI Cloud
  • Your voice, documents and answers are not stored — not even the clinic can read them
  • Every answer is signed inside the enclave; tap TEE ✓ in the assistant to check the proof yourself
